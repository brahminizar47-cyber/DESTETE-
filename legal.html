  • يلتزم المستخدم بتقديم بيانات صحيحة ومحدثة، والمحافظة على سرية بيانات الدخول، وعدم استعمال المنصة بطريقة مخالفة للقانون أو تضر بالمستخدمين أو بالمنصة.
  • يجب أن يكون للمعلن حق قانوني في نشر الإعلان والصور والبيانات المرفقة، وأن يحدّث حالة العقار عند بيعه أو كرائه أو عدم توفره.

3. Privacy Policy and Personal Data

  • Account information: email address, name, phone number and governorate when provided.
  • Listing information: property description, location, price, photos, advertiser type and listing status.
  • Feature-use information: favorites, requests to reveal contact details, reviews, reports, administrative action records and security events.
  • Local device settings such as language and certain comparison or preference data needed for site functionality.

Purposes

Creating and managing accounts, displaying listings, enabling direct contact, saving favorites, managing reviews and reports, preventing misuse, protecting the platform, providing support and improving the service.

Public and non-public information

Listing information an advertiser chooses to publish is visible to visitors. Private account details and phone numbers are not intended to be publicly exposed to visitors; a phone contact is made available through the platform's contact function to signed-in users, subject to platform controls.

Sharing and service providers

Data may be processed by hosting and technical infrastructure providers needed to operate the site, including Vercel and Supabase, or disclosed to competent authorities where there is a lawful basis. We do not sell personal data. Hosting or processing may take place outside Tunisia depending on provider configurations and applicable law.

Retention and requests

Data is retained as needed to operate accounts and the service, handle disputes and reports, and meet legal and security obligations. Users may request access, correction, deletion or information through the in-platform report/complaint function. Requests are assessed under applicable law and any duties that require certain data to be retained.

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect data, but no internet-connected system can guarantee absolute security. This policy will be updated if data-processing practices change.

4. شروط نشر الإعلانات

  • يلتزم المعلن بأن تكون المعلومات والصور حديثة وصحيحة، وأن يملك حق نشرها وألا تنتحل هوية شخص آخر.
  • يجب اختيار نوع المعلن بدقة: مالك العقار أو وسيط مستقل أو وكالة عقارية. هذا التصريح صادر عن المعلن ولا يعني أن المنصة تحققت منه أو صادقت عليه.
  • يجب توضيح نوع الملكية للأراضي (فردية أو مشتركة) وطبيعتها (سكنية أو فلاحية) وفق الحقول المطلوبة.
  • يمنع نشر عقار لا يملك المعلن حق الإعلان عنه، أو استعمال صور لا تمثله، أو إخفاء عيوب جوهرية بقصد التضليل، أو نشر بيانات شخصية للغير دون سند مشروع.
  • يتحمل المعلن مسؤولية تحديث السعر والوصف والصور وحالة العقار، والرد على الاستفسارات بمعلومات صادقة.
  • يجوز للإدارة طلب معلومات إضافية أو تعليق الإعلان أو رفضه أو إزالته عند الاشتباه في مخالفة أو بناءً على بلاغ أو طلب قانوني.

4. Listing Publication Rules

  • Advertisers must ensure that listing details and photos are current and accurate, that they have the right to publish them, and that they do not impersonate another person.
  • The advertiser must accurately select their type: property owner, independent broker or real-estate agency. This is a self-declaration and does not mean the platform has verified or endorsed the advertiser.
  • Land listings must state the ownership type (individual or shared) and land classification (residential or agricultural) in the required fields.
  • Users must not list property they have no right to advertise, use photos that do not represent it, conceal material defects to mislead, or publish another person's personal data without a lawful basis.
  • Advertisers are responsible for updating price, description, photos and property status, and for responding with truthful information.
  • Administrators may request further information, suspend, reject or remove a listing where a breach is suspected, a report is received or a lawful request is made.

  • The reporting process does not prevent users from contacting competent authorities where a crime or urgent danger is suspected.
